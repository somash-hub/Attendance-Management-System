Features

Everything the institution needs

From individual student records to department-wide analytics, AttendIQ covers the full attendance lifecycle.

✓

One-click Attendance

Teachers mark present, absent, or late for every student in seconds. No paper registers, no manual entry errors.

↗

Live Analytics

Students see their attendance percentage per subject in real time. Warnings fire automatically at 75%.

▤

Leave Management

Students apply for leave with a reason and document. Teachers and admins approve or reject with one click.

◇

Role-based Access

Separate dashboards for students, teachers, and administrators - each seeing exactly what they need.

●

Smart Notifications

Automated alerts for low attendance, approved leaves, and rescheduled classes delivered instantly.

▣

Secure & Compliant

Role-gated data access ensures students only see their own records. Admins control all thresholds.

How it works

Three roles, one platform

01

Admin sets up

Configure subjects, faculty assignments, and attendance thresholds from the admin panel.

02

Teacher marks

Each session the teacher selects the date and subject, then taps each student's attendance status.

03

Student tracks

Students view their real-time percentage, request leaves, and get alerted before falling below 75%.
